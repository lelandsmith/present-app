Present

Privacy policy · Updated 22 October 2026

What Present stores

Your reflections, journal entries, and settings are stored on your iPhone. If backup is on, a copy is kept in a private database (hosted by Supabase in the United States) under an account identifier created for this install. Each reflection and entry is encrypted on your iPhone before it is backed up, with a key that only your devices hold, so neither we nor Supabase can read it. If you lose every device and your recovery key, nobody can open the backup, including us. Copies made by earlier versions of Present are replaced with encrypted ones the first time the updated app backs up; Supabase’s own short-term backups of the earlier copies expire on their regular schedule. The account identifier is random; it is not your name, email or phone number. If you link your Apple ID, Apple shares a private identifier with us so your backup can follow you to a new phone; we do not receive your email unless you choose to share it with Apple’s sign-in.

AI questions

When AI questions are on, the feelings and topics you chose and the questions and answers in the reflection you are doing (including paths you set aside), or the journal entry you are writing, are sent to Anthropic’s Claude model to write the next question, a closing summary, truths from that entry, or a message you choose to share. For a message, the relationship you pick (such as partner or friend) goes too, never the person’s name: Present takes the name out on your phone, and the people you pick are remembered on your phone only. Nothing from your other reflections or entries is included. Anthropic does not use this data to train models. If Claude is unavailable or slow to answer, the same text may be sent to OpenAI to write it instead. OpenAI does not use it to train models either. Text that suggests you may be at risk is never sent; Present shows support information instead. You can turn AI questions off in Settings, and Present then works entirely on your phone.

Speaking instead of typing

On iOS 26 and later, when you tap Speak instead, your iPhone writes out what you say itself, and the recording never leaves it. On older iPhones, or in a language the iPhone can’t write out, and only when AI questions are on, the recording is sent to OpenAI to be written out, and only the text comes back. You see a notice before the first time. Present does not keep the audio, and OpenAI does not use it to train models. We keep the length of each recording to prevent abuse.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of your data. Your writing is never added to logs. We keep a count of AI requests per account, and of requests that couldn’t be answered and why (time, type and size only), to prevent abuse and keep the service working.

Reminders and Face ID

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Face ID or Touch ID is checked by iOS; Present never sees your biometric data.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all data and account removes everything on your phone and permanently deletes your backup account and all of its records from our servers. Export all data gives you a copy first.

Children

Present is not directed at children under 13.
